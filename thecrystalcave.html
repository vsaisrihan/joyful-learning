The Crystal Cave

Nila and Dev were collecting smooth stones near the mountain when they heard music under their feet. A narrow opening glowed behind a curtain of vines. Inside, crystals covered the walls like frozen stars. Each crystal rang a soft note when light touched it. Nila held up her lantern, and the cave answered with a bright melody. At the center of the cave was a crystal shaped like a heart. It had grown dim because the cave had not heard laughter for many years. Dev told his funniest joke, and Nila laughed so hard that her lantern shook. The heart crystal began to shine pink, blue, and gold. The music grew warm and gentle. Before they left, the cave gave them two tiny crystals to remember their visit. Nila and Dev promised to return with more songs, more stories, and more laughter.
