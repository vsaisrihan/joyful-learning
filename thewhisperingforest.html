The Whispering Forest

Ellie the Elephant stepped softly onto the carpet of moss. She had never seen flowers that glowed like stars before. A tiny butterfly fluttered down and landed right on the tip of her trunk. "Welcome, Ellie," whispered the butterfly. "The forest has been waiting for someone kind." Ellie listened carefully. The leaves rustled like tiny voices, guiding her deeper between the tall trees. Soon she found a young deer standing beside a dry stream. The deer looked worried because the moonflower that gave light to the forest had closed its petals. Ellie wanted to help. She filled her trunk with cool water from a hidden spring and sprinkled it gently over the moonflower's roots. The butterfly hummed a soft tune, and the trees swayed along. Slowly, the moonflower opened. Silver light spilled across the moss, and every flower began to sparkle again. The deer smiled, the birds sang, and the forest whispered, "Thank you, Ellie." From that day on, Ellie visited the forest whenever she could. She learned that even the quietest place has a story, and even the smallest act of kindness can make the whole world shine.
