The Blue River

There was a beautiful blue river that flowed through a small village. The water was so clear that people could see colorful fish swimming below. Birds sang in the trees, and bright flowers grew along the riverbank. One sunny morning, a girl named Maya and her friend Ravi went for a walk by the river. They noticed that some trash had been left on the ground. "The river helps us every day," said Maya. "We should help it too!" The two friends picked up the litter and put it into recycling and trash bins. Soon, other children joined them. By the end of the day, the riverbank was clean and beautiful again. As they sat beside the sparkling blue river, a family of ducks swam happily across the water. "Look!" said Ravi. "The river looks even brighter now." From that day on, the children promised to keep the blue river clean. They learned that when everyone works together, nature stays healthy and beautiful for all.
