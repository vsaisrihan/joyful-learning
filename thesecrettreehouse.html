The Secret Treehouse

In a small village, there was a big old tree near the river. Two friends, Mia and Rohan, loved playing around it. One day, they noticed something hidden behind the branches. It was a small wooden tree house! They were surprised and excited. A ladder made of ropes helped them climb up. Inside, it was cozy with small chairs and drawings on the walls. They found a box full of storybooks and toys. There was a note that said, “Share joy with others.” Mia and Rohan decided to keep it a secret place. They spent many happy afternoons reading and playing there. They also shared the toys with other village children. Soon, the tree house became a place of laughter and friendship. Everyone in the village felt happy and included. Mia and Rohan learned the joy of sharing. They promised to always take care of the secret tree house.
