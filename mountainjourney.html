Mountain Journey

One bright morning, Emma and her brother Leo set out on a journey to a tall mountain. They packed water, snacks, and warm jackets. Their goal was to reach the top before sunset. As they climbed, they saw colorful flowers, tall trees, and friendly birds. They stopped to rest and enjoyed the fresh mountain air. Along the way, they helped a small turtle cross the path safely. The trail became steeper, but Emma and Leo did not give up. They encouraged each other with smiles and kind words. Step by step, they climbed higher. At last, they reached the top of the mountain. They looked down at the green valleys, shining rivers, and tiny houses below. The view was amazing, and they felt proud of their hard work. As they walked back home, they remembered that every big journey begins with one small step.
