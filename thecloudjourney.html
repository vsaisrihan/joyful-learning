The Cloud Journey

Once upon a time, a little white cloud floated happily across the bright blue sky. It loved traveling with the gentle wind and seeing the beautiful forests, rivers, mountains, and villages below. As it moved across the sky, it collected tiny drops of water and slowly became bigger and heavier. One day, the cloud noticed that the fields were dry, the flowers were wilting, and the animals were looking for water. Feeling kind, the cloud decided to help. It gently poured rain over the land, bringing fresh water to the rivers and helping the plants grow again. The farmers were happy, the animals enjoyed the cool rain, and the flowers bloomed with bright colors. When the rain stopped, the sun came out and painted a beautiful rainbow across the sky. The little cloud became light again and continued its journey, feeling proud that it had helped so many living things.
