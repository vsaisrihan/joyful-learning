The Midnight Library

Mira loved the old library at the end of Maple Street. One evening, she returned a book just as the clock began to strike twelve. Instead of closing, the library lights glowed silver, and a tiny paper boat sailed across the carpet. Mira followed it between tall shelves until she found a book with no title. When she opened it, the blank pages filled with a map of the library. The map showed a missing chapter hiding behind the history shelf. Mira gently pushed the shelf, and a secret reading room appeared. Inside, books whispered facts, riddles, and brave ideas. A small golden key waited on a desk with a note that said, "Every good reader opens a new door." Mira used the key on the untitled book, and the missing chapter flew back into place. By morning, the library looked normal again, but Mira knew its secret. From that day on, she visited often, ready for the next midnight mystery.
