The Tiny Planet

Isha had a telescope that could see farther than any telescope in town. One clear night, she saw a tiny green planet blinking beside the moon. It looked lonely, so she waved. To her surprise, the planet waved back with a ring of sparkles. The next night, Isha built a paper rocket and placed a note inside that said, "Hello, little planet." A breeze carried the rocket up, up, and away. Soon a shining seed floated down to Isha's windowsill. She planted it in a pot, and by morning it had grown a small silver leaf shaped like a star. Each night, the leaf collected moonlight and sent it back to the tiny planet. The planet grew brighter and greener. Isha learned that even a small hello can make a faraway place feel less alone.
