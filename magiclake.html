The Magic Lake

Lily found a beautiful lake hidden in a green forest. The water sparkled like tiny diamonds in the sunlight. A little golden fish jumped out and smiled at her. "Welcome to the Magic Lake," said the fish. Lily gently touched the water with her hand. Suddenly, colorful flowers bloomed all around the lake. Bright butterflies danced in the warm breeze. Friendly ducks swam happily across the clear water. A rainbow appeared above the lake. The fish gave Lily a shiny blue pebble as a gift. "Always be kind, and the magic will stay with you," the fish said. Lily thanked the fish with a big smile. She carefully kept the pebble in her pocket. When she reached home, she shared her wonderful story with her family. Everyone smiled and listened with excitement. Lily never forgot the magical day at the lake.
