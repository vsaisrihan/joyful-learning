The Night Sky

One calm evening, a little girl named Anu stepped outside and looked up at the night sky. It was dark and beautiful, filled with twinkling stars. "Mom, why do the stars sparkle?" Anu asked with wonder. Her mother smiled and said, "The stars shine brightly to make the night sky beautiful." Soon, the bright moon rose high into the sky. Its soft moonlight covered the trees, houses, and flowers. Anu began counting the stars. "One... two... three... Oh! There are too many to count!" she laughed. Just then, a bright shooting star streaked across the sky. "Look, Mom! A shooting star!" Anu exclaimed happily. Her mother said, "The night sky is full of wonderful surprises. Every night, nature gives us something beautiful to see." Anu smiled, waved to the moon and stars, and whispered, "Good night!" She went to bed with happy dreams, excited to look at the beautiful night sky again.
