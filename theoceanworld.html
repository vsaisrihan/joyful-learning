Ocean Adventure

The Ocean World

One bright morning, a little girl named Lily visited the ocean with her family. She looked at the sparkling blue water and smiled. "Wow! The ocean is so big!" Lily said. As she watched the waves, she imagined swimming under the sea. There she saw colorful fish swimming together, a friendly sea turtle gliding slowly, and a playful dolphin jumping high above the water. A tiny crab walked sideways on the sandy ocean floor. "Hello!" said the crab. "Hello!" Lily replied with a laugh. Soon, an octopus waved its eight arms, and a bright starfish rested quietly on a rock. Beautiful coral reefs were home to many sea animals. Lily remembered not to throw any trash into the ocean. "We must keep the ocean clean so the sea animals stay safe and happy," she said. As the sun began to set, the ocean sparkled like a blanket of diamonds. Lily waved goodbye to the ocean and said, "Thank you for the wonderful adventure! I'll visit again." She went home with happy memories and a promise to protect the ocean.
