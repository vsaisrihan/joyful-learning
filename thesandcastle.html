Beach Adventure

The Sand Castel

One sunny day, Mia went to the beach with her family. Mia found soft, golden sand. "I will build a big sand castle!" she said with a smile. She filled her bucket with wet sand. Pat! Pat! Pat! She turned the bucket upside down. Wow! A little sand tower appeared. Soon, Mia made more towers. She added a tiny door. She made little windows. She dug a small river around the castle. A friendly crab walked by. "Hello!" said the crab. A little seagull flew overhead. "Nice castle!" it chirped. Mia laughed happily. Then... A gentle wave came. Splash! The wave washed away one tiny tower. "Oh no!" said Mia. Dad smiled. "That's okay. We can build it again." Mia smiled too. She built an even bigger tower. This time, her whole family helped. Soon, the biggest sand castle on the beach was ready. Everyone clapped. Mia said, "Building together is the best part!" The sun began to set. Mia waved goodbye to the beach. "I'll build another sand castle next time!"
