The Starlight Train

Every Friday night, Tara heard a train whistle outside her window, but there were no tracks near her house. One night, she looked outside and saw a train made of starlight waiting beside the mango tree. The conductor tipped his blue hat and invited her aboard. The seats were soft as clouds, and the windows showed planets, comets, and sleeping towns. The train stopped at the Station of Lost Wishes. There, Tara found small glowing tickets with wishes written on them. One ticket wished for courage before a school play. Another wished for a friend at recess. Tara helped sort the tickets into bright mailbags, and the train carried them across the sky. When she returned home, a silver ticket rested in her pocket. It said, "Kind helpers always have a seat." Tara kept it safely under her pillow.
