Rainy Day Fun

It was a rainy morning, and dark clouds covered the sky. Little Emma looked out of the window with a sad face. She wanted to play outside with her friends. Her mother smiled and gave her a bright yellow raincoat. Emma wore her boots and carried a colorful umbrella. She stepped outside and heard the soft sound of the rain. She happily splashed in little puddles. Her brother joined her with a paper boat. They floated their boats in the small stream beside the road. Tiny frogs jumped, and birds sang after the rain slowed down. Soon, a beautiful rainbow appeared in the sky. Emma pointed at it with excitement. She laughed and played until it was time to go home. Her mother made warm soup for everyone. Emma realized that even rainy days could be full of joy. She thanked her mother for making the day special.
