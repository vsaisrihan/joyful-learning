Moonlight Adventure

Little Moon Boat

Nila made a little paper boat and placed it on the river at night. The moon shone so brightly that the boat looked silver. It floated past sleepy reeds, quiet stones, and fireflies blinking like tiny lamps. Nila walked beside it until it reached a bend in the river. There, the boat stopped near a lost toy star. Nila picked up the star and promised to return it to the child who missed it. The moon seemed to smile, and the little boat sailed home with her.
