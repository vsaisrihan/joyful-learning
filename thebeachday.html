Beach Day Fun

One sunny morning, Mia and her family went to the beach. The soft sand felt warm under her feet. She built a beautiful sandcastle with her little brother. They collected colorful seashells along the shore. The waves splashed gently as they laughed and played. Mia noticed some trash lying on the sand. She picked it up and put it in a nearby bin. Her brother helped her clean the beach too. Soon, other children joined them. The beach became clean and beautiful again. A family of seagulls landed happily near the water. Everyone enjoyed the fresh sea breeze. They watched the bright sun slowly set over the ocean. The sky turned orange, pink, and purple. Mia smiled because she had helped protect nature. She promised to always keep beaches clean.
