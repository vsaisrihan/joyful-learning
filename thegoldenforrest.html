The Golden Forest

Emma loved exploring the beautiful Golden Forest near her village. Every morning, the trees shone like gold in the warm sunlight. One day, Emma heard a tiny bird calling for help. She followed the sound and found a little bird with a hurt wing. Emma gently picked up the bird and cared for it. She gave it water and kept it safe under a big tree. Soon, the bird felt stronger and began to fly again. As a thank-you, the bird showed Emma a hidden path. The path led to a garden filled with colorful flowers. Butterflies danced happily around the bright blossoms. Emma smiled as she enjoyed the peaceful place. She picked up litter to keep the forest clean. The animals seemed happy to see her kindness. Before going home, Emma thanked the forest for its beauty. She promised to protect nature every day. The Golden Forest became her favorite place to visit.
