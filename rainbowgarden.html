Garden Adventure

Rainbow Garden

Tara found a quiet garden behind her school where every flower had a different color. When the sun came out after the rain, the flowers opened one by one and made a bright rainbow path. Tara followed it carefully and found a tiny dry patch of soil. She shared water from her bottle, and the smallest seed began to grow. By evening, a new violet flower bloomed. Tara smiled because she learned that gardens grow best when everyone gives a little care.
