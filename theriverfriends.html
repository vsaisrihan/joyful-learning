The Three River Friends

In a beautiful jungle, a clear river flowed gently. Near the river lived three best friends—Ellie the Elephant, Benny the Bunny, and Monty the Monkey. Every morning, they met by the river to play, laugh, and enjoy the fresh air. One hot day, many animals came to the river because they were thirsty. Ellie filled her trunk with cool water and gently sprayed it on the smaller animals to help them cool down. Monty climbed a tall tree and picked sweet fruits for everyone to share. Benny happily gathered colorful flowers and showed his friends a lovely spot by the river. Just then, a little deer stood at the edge of the river. It was afraid to cross. "Don't worry," said Ellie. "We'll help you!" Ellie carefully guided the little deer through the shallow water. Monty showed the safest path from a tree, and Benny stayed beside the deer, cheering it on. Soon, the little deer reached the other side safely. "Thank you, my friends!" the deer said with a big smile. As the sun began to set, the three friends sat by the river and watched the golden sky together. They learned that helping one another makes friendship stronger and every day more joyful.
