The Clockwork Kite

Ravi found a broken kite in his grandfather's workshop. It had wooden wings, brass wheels, and a little clock on its tail. "This kite only flies when someone asks a brave question," Grandfather said with a wink. Ravi repaired the string, turned the tiny key, and asked, "Where does the wind sleep?" The kite lifted into the air and tugged him across the meadow. It carried him over rooftops, fields, and a sleepy train station until they reached a hill where the wind rested inside tall silver grass. Ravi listened carefully. The wind told him it worked all day pushing clouds, spinning seeds, and cooling tired travelers. Ravi thanked the wind and asked the kite to take him home. When he returned, the clock on the kite chimed once. Ravi wrote his question in a notebook, then smiled. Tomorrow, he would ask something even braver.
